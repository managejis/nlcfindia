Tuition Program

Where We Teach

Our Three Tuition Centres

Three centres, three communities, one purpose: to make sure that no child is left behind because of the circumstances they were born into. Together our centres support 60 children.

Chikkagubbi, Bangalore

Chikkagubbi Tuition Centre

25 children

Chikkagubbi is our largest tuition centre, with 25 children. It is close to the foundation's own office at Unnathi Layout.

Here the children sit together on mats around their teacher, with their books and notebooks in front of them. The class works through the day's school lessons and homework together, and the teacher moves among the children to explain what they find difficult and to check that each one has understood.

For these children, this is a place where someone sits down with them and goes through their schoolwork. Along with the lessons, the children are taught basic computer knowledge, spoken English and moral values such as honesty, respect and kindness.

Because the group is large and the children are of different ages and classes, our teachers give special attention to the younger and slower learners so that nobody is left behind.

Shikaripura, Shivamogga District

Shikaripura Tuition Centre

20 children

Our Shikaripura centre in Shivamogga district serves 20 children and shows that the tuition program is not limited to the city: it reaches children in a smaller town in rural Karnataka, where good academic support outside school is harder to find.

The children come together in a simple hall, where they sit in rows on mats and work in their notebooks while the teacher guides the class. Learning the letters and writing, reading, and completing school homework are the heart of the day.

The children are of different ages and school classes, and they study side by side, helping one another. The teacher explains the lessons patiently and encourages every child to ask questions, which builds confidence in children who may hesitate to speak up in a crowded school classroom.

Alongside the lessons, we encourage regular school attendance and good habits.

Budigere, Bangalore

Budigere Tuition Centre

15 children

The Budigere centre is a small, close-knit group of 15 children. The class meets in a home-like room where the children sit together on the floor with their school bags and notebooks, with our teachers sitting right beside them.

The small size of the group allows a great deal of personal attention. Teachers and volunteers can sit with each child, check their homework, correct mistakes gently and explain a lesson again until it is understood.

The children at Budigere come from underprivileged families. Here they receive regular guidance in their school subjects, an introduction to computers, spoken English practice and moral education, all in a warm and friendly atmosphere that makes them look forward to coming.

The centre also gives the children a safe and encouraging place to learn, which helps them stay interested in school and grow in confidence.

Education for Every Child

A Safe Place to Learn, Grow and Dream

Education is one of the most powerful tools for transforming a life. Many children from underprivileged families go to school but have no one at home to guide them with homework, explain difficult lessons or encourage them to keep going. Without that support they fall behind and are at risk of dropping out.

New Life Charitable Foundation runs tuition centres where children receive daily academic support in a caring, family-like atmosphere. Our teachers and volunteers sit with the children, work patiently with each one, and help them understand their lessons so that they can perform better in school and grow in confidence.

Today our tuition program reaches 60 children through three tuition centres: Chikkagubbi (25 children), Shikaripura in Shivamogga district (20 children) and Budigere (15 children).
